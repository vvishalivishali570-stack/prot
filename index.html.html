About Us

Mini Candy Shop is a home-grown candy studio created by K. Vishali, a passionate Digital Marketer who turned her love for sweets and design into a full-time creative venture.

What started as a small hobby of making candy favors for friends and family has grown into a shop known for its colorful, Instagram-worthy treats and attention to detail.

We believe every celebration deserves a little extra sweetness — from birthday parties and baby showers to weddings and festive gifting, our candies are made to bring smiles and add color to your special moments.
